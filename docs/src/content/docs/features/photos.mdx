On the Echo Shows.

Photos from your [Immich](https://immich.app) library and from shared Google Photos albums can show
in three places:

- **Wallpaper**: behind the clock on the native dashboard, changing on a schedule
- **Screen saver**: pick **Photos** for a screen saver visual slot
- **Photos app**: full screen, from the Apps screen

<Image src={photoApp} alt="The full-screen Photos app showing a Google Photos sailing image in Framed fit" />

Photo settings are on the device under **Settings → Display → Photos**, and in Home Assistant on
the device's page. Turn on Immich, Google Photos or both; with both on, photos come from each.

<Image src={settings} alt="On-screen photo settings for wallpaper, the Photos app, fit, transitions and library providers" />

## Connect Immich

<Steps>

1. In Immich, create an API key: **Account settings → API keys**. See
   [API key permissions](#api-key-permissions) for which permissions it needs.

2. On the device, open **Settings → Display → Photos → Immich**, turn on **Enable**, and set
   **Server** to Immich's address, for example `http://192.168.1.20:2283`, and **API key** to the key.
   In Home Assistant these are **Immich photos** (`photos_immich_enabled`), **Immich server**
   (`photos_immich_server`) and **Immich API key** (`photos_immich_key`).

3. Narrow down which photos to show, or leave these empty for the whole library:

   | Setting | Entity | |
   |---|---|---|
   | Albums | `photos_immich_albums` | Album names or ids, separated by commas |
   | Tags | `photos_immich_tags` | Tag names or ids, separated by commas |
   | People | `photos_immich_people` | People's names or ids, separated by commas |
   | Favorites only | `photos_immich_favorites` | Only favorites (default off) |
   | Memories first | `photos_immich_memories` | Prefer Immich's "on this day" memories (default on) |
   | Quality | `photos_immich_quality` | **Preview** (default) or **Full size** |

   Names are case-insensitive. Within one setting, any match counts: two albums selects photos from either.
   A name Immich doesn't know shows as "Nothing named …" in the status.

4. Turn on **Settings → Display → Photos → Show wallpaper**.

</Steps>

The **Status** row on the Immich page, and **Photos Immich status** (`photos_immich_status`) in
Home Assistant, say whether the connection works and what's wrong if it doesn't. Immich 3.2 or
newer is needed.

<Image src={immich} alt="Immich settings: server, API key, albums, tags, people, favourites, memories and quality" />

## API key permissions

Immich API keys can be limited to specific permissions. The device only reads from Immich; it
never changes anything. A key needs:

| Permission | Needed when |
|---|---|
| `server.about` | Always |
| `asset.read` | Always |
| `asset.view` | Always |
| `memory.read` | **Memories first** is on |
| `album.read` | **Albums** is filled in |
| `tag.read` | **Tags** is filled in |
| `person.read` | **People** is filled in |
| `face.read` | Optional: Fill and Slow pan crop around faces |

With no filters and **Memories first** off, the first three are enough. A key with all permissions
also works.

If the key is missing a permission the current settings need, the status names it, for example
"API key needs memory.read". "Wrong API key" means Immich refused the key itself.

The key can access only its Immich user's photos. For restricted access, create it under a user
with only the intended albums shared. The **Albums** setting filters which photos are displayed
without restricting the key's access.

## Google Photos

Google Photos albums are read from their share links; no Google account is signed in on the
device.

<Steps>

1. In Google Photos, open the album, tap **Share** and create a link. It looks like
   `https://photos.app.goo.gl/…`.

2. On the device, open **Settings → Display → Photos → Google Photos**, turn on **Enable**, and
   paste the link into **Album 1 link**.

3. Add up to three more albums in **Album 2 link** to **Album 4 link**.

</Steps>

Each album's **Share** (0–10, default 5) sets how often its photos come up compared with the other
albums: an album at 10 shows up twice as often as one at 5. At 0 the album is left out. Albums
refresh every 6 hours to include new photos.

| Setting | Entity | |
|---|---|---|
| Enable | `switch.<device>_screen_google_photos` | Off by default |
| Album 1 link … Album 4 link | `text.<device>_screen_google_photos_album_1_link` … `text.<device>_screen_google_photos_album_4_link` | Share links |
| Album 1 share … Album 4 share | `number.<device>_screen_google_photos_album_1_share` … `number.<device>_screen_google_photos_album_4_share` | 0–10, default 5 |

The **Status** row on the Google Photos page, and **Photos Google Photos status**
(`photos_google_status`) in Home Assistant, say whether the albums could be read.

## Screen saver

Pick **Photos** as **Visual 1** or **Visual 2** under **Settings → Display → Screen saver**. Three
more settings then appear on that page:

| Setting | Entity | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Next photo every | `select.<device>_screen_screen_saver_next_photo_every` | 10 s, 15 s, 30 s, 1 min, 2 min, 5 min | 30 s |
| Photo fit | `select.<device>_screen_screen_saver_photo_fit` | See [Fit](#fit) | Slow pan |
| Transition | `select.<device>_screen_screen_saver_photo_transition` | See [Transition](#transition) | Fade |

## Photos app

**Photos** on the Apps screen shows your photos full screen. Tap the left or right edge for the
previous or next photo. The corner buttons close it and open the photo settings.

| Setting | Entity | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photos app next photo every | `select.<device>_screen_photos_app_next_photo_every` | 5 s, 10 s, 15 s, 30 s, 1 min, 2 min, 5 min, Never | 15 s |
| Photos app fit | `select.<device>_screen_photos_app_fit` | See [Fit](#fit) | Slow pan |
| Photos app transition | `select.<device>_screen_photos_app_transition` | See [Transition](#transition) | Fade |

## Fit

The wallpaper, the screen saver's photo slots (`idle_photo_fit`) and the Photos app
(`photos_app_fit`) each have their own **Fit**:

| Fit | Shows |
|---|---|
| Auto | Fill when the photo's shape is close to the screen's, otherwise Fit, blurred edges |
| Fill | Fills the screen, cropping what doesn't fit |
| Fit, blurred edges | The whole photo, over a blurred, darkened copy of itself |
| Fit, plain edges | The whole photo, on the theme's background |
| Framed | The whole photo in a light mat with a shadow, over a blurred copy |
| Slow pan | Fills the screen and slowly zooms and pans across the photo, turning back every 30 seconds |

With Immich and a key that has `face.read`, Fill and Slow pan keep the faces Immich found in view
instead of cropping around the center.

The wallpaper defaults to Auto; the screen saver and the Photos app default to Slow pan.

## Transition

Wallpaper, screen saver and the Photos app each have a transition setting: `poster_transition`,
`idle_photo_transition` and `photos_app_transition`.

| Transition | Shows |
|---|---|
| None | The next photo replaces the last at once |
| Fade | The last photo fades into the next |
| Slide | The next photo slides in from the right |
| Zoom | The next photo grows into place as the last fades |
| Wipe | A soft edge sweeps across from left to right |
| Dissolve | The next photo appears in scattered specks |

All three default to Fade.

<Image src={transition} alt="Every photo transition: None, Fade, Slide, Zoom, Wipe and Dissolve" />

[Config sync](/use/configuration/#config-sync) copies the Immich API key along with the other photo settings. Home Assistant shows the key only as set or not set.
