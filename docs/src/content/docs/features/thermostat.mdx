On the Echo Shows.

The **Thermostat** app shows up to four Home Assistant `climate` entities: the temperature inside,
humidity, what the system is doing, and the set point. With two or more, they share the screen.

<Image src={thermostat} alt="Two thermostats side by side in the Dial look with rehearsal data" />

## Set it up

Open **Settings → Home control → Thermostat** and turn on the ones to show under **Thermostats**,
up to four. Enable home control and **Allow the device to perform Home Assistant actions** in the
device's ESPHome integration options.

## Using it

- Drag the set point, or press **−** and **+**. On a thermostat in Auto with a low and a high set
  point, both can be moved.
- **Mode**, **Preset** and **Fan** open a chooser with the options the thermostat offers.
- The status reads **Heating to**, **Cooling to**, **Keeping between**, **Holding at**, **Drying**
  or **Fan running**.
- The outside temperature comes from the weather entity under
  [Home control → Weather](/use/settings/#weather).

## Looks

**Look** (`thermostat_look`) picks one of 8 designs: Dial (the default), Column, Wheel, Bars,
Split, Terminal, Brass and Mission. The picker shows each one with your first thermostat.

The examples use rehearsal data.

## Settings

| Setting | Entity | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thermostats | — | The Home Assistant `climate` entities to show, up to four | |
| Look | `select.<device>_screen_thermostat_look` | The design | Dial |
| Dashboard tab | `switch.<device>_screen_thermostat_dashboard_tab` | A **Thermostat** tab on the [native dashboard](/use/dashboards/) | Off |
