Wake-word detection runs on the device. Home Assistant processes speech and replies through
the selected Assist pipeline.

## Two assistants

**Assistant 1** and **Assistant 2** each have their own wake word, Assist pipeline, sensitivity,
sounds and timing. Assign different pipelines to the two wake words, including local and cloud pipelines.
A slot with no wake word is off.

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. In Home Assistant, open the device's **Assistant 1** device.

2. Pick an **Assistant** (Assist pipeline) and a **Wake word**. Custom wake words added to Home
   Assistant are listed too. The [EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/) adds a wake word library for managing them.

3. Repeat for **Assistant 2** to configure the second wake word and pipeline.

</Steps>

On an Echo Show you can also pick the wake word under **Settings → Voice assistants**. That needs
**Allow the device to perform Home Assistant actions** turned on for the device in the ESPHome
integration; without it the row is dimmed.

## More wake words

Turn these on under **Settings → Voice assistants** on an Echo Show to add
more choices to each assistant's **Wake word** list:

| Toggle | Adds |
|---|---|
| **Amazon wake words** | Alexa, Amazon, Computer, Echo, Ziggy, Hey Disney, Hey Melissa, Hey Samuel, Hey Shaq and Amit Ji, in several languages and dialects: **Pryon: Alexa**, **Pryon: Alexa (de-DE)** |
| **Gemini wake word** | "Hey Google", listed as **Gemini: Hey Google** |

A word is downloaded the first time an assistant uses it.

## Starting without the wake word

- **Echo Dot:** tap the action button for assistant 1, hold it for assistant 2.
- **Echo Show:** **Talk now** on the assistant's settings page.
- From Home Assistant: the **Wake** button on the assistant's device (`wake_assistant_1`,
  `wake_assistant_2`).

## On the screen

On the Echo Shows,
**Settings → Voice assistants** has a page per assistant:

- **Talk now**
- **Wake word**
- **Wake word sensitivity**: higher wakes more easily, with more false wakes. In Home Assistant the
  same setting is a threshold (0.5–0.99, default 0.85) where *lower* wakes more easily
- **Wake sound**: a chime, a [custom tone](/features/tones/), or none
- **Response playback**: **Stream** starts sooner and can stutter on a slow network; **Download
  first** waits for the whole answer
- **Visuals**: a visual for listening, thinking and replying, in the panel or full screen

## Timing, follow-up and recordings

Find these in Home Assistant on each assistant's device, in the
[EchoLocal card](/start/integration/#the-card)'s assistant panel, or in the device's
[web page](/use/web/):

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Follow-up time** | Keep listening after a reply, so you can ask again without the wake word (0–30 s) | 0 |
| **Max listening time** | Stop listening after this long (5–60 s) | 15 s |
| **Max thinking time** | Timeout for a Home Assistant response (5–300 s) | 90 s |
| **Reply buffer** | How much of a streamed reply to collect before playing (0–3000 ms) | 650 ms |
| **Recordings kept** | Keep the audio of the last N requests (0–10), for the integration's Activity tab | 0 |

The **Last wake word**, **Last heard** and **Last reply** sensors show the most recent turn.

On the Echo Dot, the ring shows each stage with its own effect: **Wake word effect**, **Thinking
effect** and **Replying effect**, per assistant. See [Echo Dot: buttons and light
ring](/use/dot/).

## Stop

Say **"stop"**, no wake word needed, tap the screen on an Echo Show, or tap the action button on an Echo Dot. It stops, in order: a ringing alarm or timer, the assistant, an announcement, then pauses music.

**Stop word sensitivity** in Home Assistant sets the detection threshold (0.5–1, default 0.7).
Set it to 1 to disable stop-word detection.

**Amazon stop word**, under **Settings → Voice assistants**, adds Amazon's "stop" detector next to
the built-in one. It can be turned on only while **Amazon wake words** is on.

## Voice triggers

Two kinds of things you can say, each with its own switches under **Settings → Voice triggers** on
an Echo Show and on the **voice triggers** device in Home Assistant. Everything is on to start
with.

**Actions** are said to an assistant, after the wake word. Assist hands them to the device:

- **"Call the kitchen"**, **"video call the kitchen"**: [an intercom call](/features/intercom/#calling-by-voice)
- **"Broadcast"**, **"broadcast dinner is ready"**: [a broadcast](/features/intercom/#broadcasts)

Actions need the [EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/). When it's missing or out of date,
the Voice triggers page says so, with a code to scan that opens it in HACS.

**Commands** are heard on the device itself, without a wake word (Echo Show):

- **"Radar"** (or "show me the weather radar") opens the [Radar app](/features/apps/#radar)
- **"Mirror"** opens [Mirror](/features/apps/#mirror)

**All actions** and **All commands** turn a whole kind off; each one has its own switch too.

## Alarm and call controls

These actions are spoken after the wake word and need the companion Home Assistant integration.
They also work on the Echo Dot.

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| "Set an alarm for seven thirty AM" | Saves an enabled one-time alarm |
| "Set an alarm called Work for seven thirty AM every weekday" | Saves a named repeating alarm |
| "List my alarms" | Reads the saved alarms, including disabled ones |
| "When is my next alarm?" | Reads the next enabled alarm and when it will ring |
| "Disable the Work alarm", "enable the Work alarm" | Turns that saved alarm off or on |
| "Delete the Work alarm", "delete the seven thirty AM alarm" | Deletes the matching saved alarm |
| "Snooze", "snooze my alarm" | Snoozes the ringing alarm for its saved snooze duration |
| "Stop my alarm", "dismiss the alarm" | Stops the current alarm without deleting its schedule |
| "Answer the call" | Answers the incoming call with audio only |
| "Answer the video call" | Answers with video when the incoming call supports it |
| "Hang up", "end the call", "decline the call" | Ends, cancels or declines the current call |

Use AM/PM or a 24-hour time such as `19:30`. A one-time alarm rings at the next occurrence of that
time. Repeats accept **every weekday**, **every weekend**, **every day**, or named weekdays such as
**on Monday and Friday**. New alarms have a five-minute snooze. Change their sound, snooze and other
options in the alarm editor.

Existing alarm names or identical schedules are not overwritten. Alarm changes match a saved name
or time; if more than one matches, use its name. **Next** also works: "delete the next alarm".

Each action family has its own switch under **Voice triggers**: **Manage alarms**, **Answer a call**
and **Hang up a call**. To use voice hang-up during a connected call, turn off
**Pause wake word during calls**. With it on, use the screen, buttons or HA instead.

Home Assistant can also call `esphome.<node>_call_answer` with `video: false` for audio-only or
`video: true` for video, and `esphome.<node>_call_hangup` with no arguments. These act on the current
call, including calls between devices.
