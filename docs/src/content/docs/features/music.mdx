The device is a Home Assistant **media player** called **Speaker**: radio, media sources, TTS
announcements and Music Assistant. Music ducks while the assistant listens and talks; **Music
ducking** (`media_duck_level`, −40 to −3 dB, default −15) in Home Assistant sets how far.

**Speaker EQ** (`speaker_eq`, on by default) applies the Echo's vendor speaker tuning, adjusted for
volume. Both generations of Echo Show 8 play in stereo.

## Sendspin

Sendspin provides synchronized playback with
[Music Assistant](https://music-assistant.io).

1. Run Music Assistant on the same network and turn on **Settings → Connected services → Sendspin**
   on the device, or **Sendspin** (`sendspin`) in Home Assistant.
2. Select the device by its name in Music Assistant's players and play an album, playlist or
   radio station. Create the synchronized group in Music Assistant, not in the device's Settings.
3. On a screen, open **Media** for the track, artist, album and cover art. Play/pause, previous and
   next send commands back to the Sendspin server; seeking is not offered for Sendspin playback.

With the [EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/) and Home Assistant's
[Music Assistant integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/music_assistant/),
**Media** also lists the tracks coming up in the Music Assistant queue. Tap one to play it.

**Sendspin state** (`sendspin_state`) in Home Assistant helps check the connection:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `off` | Sendspin is disabled |
| `waiting` | Enabled and waiting for a server |
| `joined` | Connected to a server |
| `playing` | The server has started playback |

The state is a connection/playback diagnostic; a paused group can retain the `playing` state.
If it stays at `waiting`, check discovery between Music Assistant and the device. Turning
**Sendspin** off disconnects it and removes its network advertisement.

## Music app (Echo Show)

**Music** in [Apps](/features/apps/) controls Music Assistant on any of its speakers, not only the
device. It needs the [EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/) and Home Assistant's
[Music Assistant integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/music_assistant/); without
the first, the app shows **Update the EchoLocal integration in Home Assistant**.

| Tab | |
|---|---|
| **Playing** | The chosen speaker's track, controls, volume and queue |
| **Home** | **Recently played**, then Music Assistant's recommendations |
| **Search** | Artists, albums, tracks, playlists and radio stations |
| **Library** | Your **Playlists**, **Albums**, **Artists** and **Radio** |
| **Speakers** | Every Music Assistant speaker, with **Find a speaker** |

Open an album, playlist or artist for its tracks, with **Play**, **Play next**, **Add to queue**
and **Similar**. On **Speakers**, tap a speaker to control it, set its volume, **Group** it with the
chosen speaker or **Move here** to bring the queue over. LANovo and EchoLocal devices show their
own picture while they're idle.

## AirPlay

On the Echo Shows.
Mirror a Mac, iPhone or iPad's screen and sound. Turn on **AirPlay**, then on the Mac or phone open
**Screen Mirroring** and pick the device by its name. The picture is sized to fit the screen and
turns with it. Stop mirroring from the Mac or phone, or turn AirPlay off.

## Chromecast

Turn on **Cast receiver**, open the Cast menu in a supported app, and select the device by name.

Echo Shows support audio and video. The Echo Dot supports YouTube and YouTube Music audio only.

YouTube skips [SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app) segments; pick which kinds under
**Cast receiver → Skip segments**, listed on [Settings on the screen](/use/settings/#cast-receiver).

## Bluetooth speaker

On the Echo Shows.
Pair the device in your phone's Bluetooth settings. Track information, cover art and playback
controls appear in **Media**.

## Sound machine

Play two background sounds together using **White noise layer 1** and **layer 2** in Home Assistant
(`noise_layer_1`, `noise_layer_2`). Choose None, White, Pink, Brown, Rain, Ocean, Brook, Wind, Fire,
Crickets, Fan or Cabin for each. Other audio reduces the background volume. Background sounds stop
when the device restarts.

## Player (Echo Show)

A mini player shows on the dashboard while something plays. Tap it, or **Media** in the dock, for
the full player. Cast and AirPlay video play full screen; tap for controls.

**Settings → Dashboard → Close paused player after** closes a paused player.
