reorder. **Media** and **Intercom** are in the dock to start with. Most apps close after three

## Media

Open **Media** for the full player: artwork, track information, playback controls and volume.
The mini-player keeps playback controls available while you use other screens. See
[Music and casting](/features/music/) for Home Assistant playback, Sendspin, Bluetooth and casting.

## Alarms

Create one-time or repeating alarms, choose a sound, set snooze and volume, or add a wake light.
The saved list is shared with the [browser alarm page](/use/web/#alarms) and the
[alarm voice commands](/features/voice/#alarm-and-call-controls).

<Image src={alarms} alt="Alarm editor with time, repeat, sound, snooze, volume and wake-light settings" />

## Intercom

The directory shows other LANovo and EchoLocal devices, with audio and video call buttons where
available. Open a device's information for more detail. See [Intercom](/features/intercom/) for
calls, broadcasts, auto-answer and browser calling.

## Radio

Stations come from the **Radio Browser** integration, which you add in Home Assistant first. The
radio opens on **Local** and **Popular** stations; star a station to put it on the dial.

The dial has playback and next/previous controls. **Local stations** and **Popular** open the
station browsers; choosing a station starts playback.

<Image src={radio} alt="The Radio app's Popular station browser" />

## Radar

An animated loop of rain and clouds around your home, with a short-range forecast. Under
**Settings → Dashboard → Weather**:

- **Radar source**: **Auto**, **NWS** (US only) or **RainViewer**
- **Radar starting range**: 50 to 800 miles, default 200

Saying "radar" opens it, unless it's turned off in the [voice triggers](/features/voice/#voice-triggers).

## Calendar

Pick the calendars and **Days to show** (3, 7 or 14) under **Settings → Home control → Calendar**.

The app displays upcoming events from those Home Assistant calendars. Calendar selection and the
date range control what appears; event editing stays in your calendar service.

## Shopping list

Pick the to-do list under **Settings → Home control → Shopping list**. Add items with the on-screen
keyboard, tap to check them off, and turn on **Show completed items** to see the checked ones.

The list is shared with Home Assistant, not stored separately on the display. A phone can type
into **Add an item** through the [QR keyboard](/use/settings/#typing-with-a-phone).

<Image src={shopping} alt="Shopping list with an Add an item field and unchecked list items" />

## Lights and switches

These open the home-control lists full screen. Choose which entities appear under **Settings →
Home control**. The same selection feeds their [native dashboard tabs](/use/dashboards/#lights-and-switches).

## Photos

Browse the configured photo source full screen. The source can also supply wallpaper behind the
clock. See [Photos and wallpaper](/features/photos/) for Immich and Google Photos, fit modes and
transitions.

## Doorbell

Open the doorbell view and recent events. Notification behaviour and which doorbells appear are
configured under **Settings → Home control → Doorbell**. See [Doorbell](/features/doorbell/).

## Home alarm

Open the selected Home Assistant alarm panel, including its zones, keypad and supported actions.
The [Home alarm guide](/features/home-alarm/) shows the panel and every design in its picker.
Opening the app does not arm or disarm the panel.

## TV

The YouTube TV channel guide, and a remote for whatever YouTube TV is playing. It needs the
[YouTube TV integration](https://github.com/ygelfand/yttv-hacs) in Home Assistant, version 0.1.12 or
newer, and **Allow the device to perform Home Assistant actions** turned on for the device in the
ESPHome integration.

<div class="pair even">
<Image src={tv} alt="The TV app: the TVs along the top, then the channel guide with a red line at the time now" />
<Image src={tvCard} alt="A channel's card: the live picture, what's next, a favorite star and Watch here" />
</div>

- **The TVs** run along the top: every Chromecast the integration knows, with the device itself
  as **Here**. Tap one to pick it.
- **Below them** is what that TV is playing, with play/pause, stop and volume.
- **The guide** lists every channel, favorites first with a star, then A to Z. A line marks the
  time now. **Find a channel or show** narrows it.
- **Tap a show** that's on now to watch it on the chosen TV.
- **Tap a channel's name** for its card: what's on and what's next, a star to make it a favorite,
  and **Watch on** the chosen TV.

**Settings → Home control → YouTube TV**, or the settings button in the app, picks the
**Default TV** the app opens on and your **Favorites**.

## YouTube

Browse and watch YouTube on the device itself, signed in to your own account. It needs nothing
from Home Assistant.

**Sign in** the first time you open it: go to **youtube.com/activate** on your phone or computer and
enter the code on the screen, or scan the QR code. Each device signs in on its own. Add more
accounts under **Accounts**, and pick which one the app uses there.

| Tab | |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Type a search; results come back as rows, with Shorts in their own row |
| **Home** | Your YouTube home feed, in rows |
| **Shorts** | Shorts YouTube picks for your account, as a wall of pictures with their titles |
| **Subscriptions** | The latest videos from the channels you subscribe to, with their Shorts on top |
| **History** | What you've watched |
| **Watch later** | Your Watch later list |
| **Accounts** | Your accounts |

- **Rows scroll sideways**; swiping to the end of a row loads more of it. Scrolling to the bottom of
  a page loads more rows.
- **A red bar** along the bottom of a picture shows how much of it you've watched, and a partly watched video
  picks up where you left off.
- **Tap a video** to play it full screen. Swipe up for the next video and down for the previous one.
- **Tap a Short** and the Shorts that follow play one after another.

Videos skip the same [SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app) segments as casting, picked under
**Cast receiver → Skip segments** on [Settings on the screen](/use/settings/#cast-receiver).

## Plex

Browse your Plex Media Server and watch movies and shows on the device itself. It needs nothing
from Home Assistant.

**Sign in** the first time you open it: go to **plex.tv/link** on your phone or computer and enter
the code on the screen, or scan the QR code. If your account has a Plex Home, pick who's watching
(and enter their PIN if they have one). Each device signs in on its own.

The bar down the side has **Search**, **Home**, one entry per library on your server, and
**Settings**. Tap the entry you're already on to reload it and go back to the top.

<Image src={plexHome} alt="Plex home rows: Continue Watching and On Deck, with See all" />

| Page | |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Search your server; results come back in rows |
| **Home** | Your server's home rows: Continue Watching, On Deck, recently added and the rest |
| A library | **Recommended** rows for that library, the whole **Library**, and **Categories** |

- **Recommended** shows the library's own rows from Plex. A library Plex has no rows for opens on
  **Library**.
- **Library** lists every title. Pick the order under **Sort**, flip it with the arrow next to it,
  and narrow it to **Unwatched**. **Shuffle** plays a random title. In title order, the letters down
  the side jump straight to that letter. More titles load as you scroll.
- **Categories** shows the library's genres; tap one to list just that genre.
- **See all** next to a row opens everything in it.
- **A bar** along the bottom of a picture shows how much you've watched.

<Image src={plexLibrary} alt="A Plex library sorted by title, with the sort menu, Unwatched, Shuffle and the letters down the side" />

<Image src={plexCategories} alt="A Plex library's categories as tiles" />

Tap a title to open it. There you can **Resume** where you left off or **Play from start**, pick
**Subtitles** (or turn them off), and **Mark watched** or **Mark unwatched**. The subtitle you pick
is saved with your account, so your other Plex apps use it too. Shows list their seasons and
episodes.

<Image src={plexTitle} alt="A Plex episode with Resume, Play from start, the Subtitles menu open and Mark watched" />

Plex decides how each title plays: it plays the file as it is when the device can decode it, and
otherwise converts what's needed on the fly. Your progress goes back to Plex as you watch, so you can
pick up on another screen.

**Settings** in the bar shows the connection status, and has **Switch profile** and **Sign out**.

## Mirror

The camera, full screen. It's on devices with a camera. Saying "mirror" opens it, unless it's
turned off in the [voice triggers](/features/voice/#voice-triggers).

## Cameras

The cameras in Home Assistant, as pictures on a grid, sorted by area. Swipe for more pages. Tap one
to watch it full screen: live video with a sound button when Home Assistant can stream that camera
and the [EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/) is installed, otherwise a picture that keeps
updating. Tap again to go back.

It needs **Allow the device to perform Home Assistant actions** turned on for the device in the
ESPHome integration. Set it up under **Settings → Home control → Cameras**:

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard tab** | Adds a **Cameras** tab to the [native dashboard](/use/dashboards/#cameras) | Off |
| **Select** | Which cameras to show | All cameras |
| **Thumbnail size** | 1 (small) to 5 (one camera per row) | 3 |
| **Refresh** | How often the pictures update, 1 to 60 s, or Off | 5 s |
| **Auto paginate** | Move to the next page on its own, 5 to 120 s, or Off | Off |
| **Home Assistant URL** | Where to fetch the pictures from. Leave it empty to use the address the device finds on your network | Empty |
