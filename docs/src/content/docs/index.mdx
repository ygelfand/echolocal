EchoLocal uses the Echo's existing microphones, speaker and buttons, plus the screen and camera on
Shows. It connects to Home Assistant through ESPHome and shares its software with
[LANovo](https://www.lanovo.link).

## Supported hardware

| Model | Generation | Codename |
|---|---|---|
| Echo Dot | 2nd gen | **biscuit** |
| Echo | 2nd gen | **radar** (experimental) |
| Echo Show 5 | 1st gen | **checkers** |
| Echo Show 5 | 2nd gen | **cronos** |
| Echo Show 8 | 1st gen | **crown** |
| Echo Show 8 | 2nd gen | **athena** |

Other models aren't supported. Unlocking differs by model and generation.
[Hardware details and unlock guides →](/start/devices/)

## Voice, audio and intercom on Dot and Show

- **[Assist](/features/voice/):** on-device wake-word detection, independent Home Assistant
  pipelines, custom wake words and feedback, and a stop word without a wake word. Pipelines can be
  local or cloud-based.
- **[Music](/features/music/):** Home Assistant media playback, Music Assistant through Sendspin,
  custom tones and background sounds. Shows also have a Bluetooth speaker mode and AirPlay
  mirroring; Chromecast is experimental. Dot casting supports YouTube and YouTube Music audio.
- **[Calls and broadcasts](/features/intercom/):** audio intercom across EchoLocal and LANovo,
  browser calling, configurable auto-answer and recorded household announcements.
- **[Timers and alarms](/features/messages/):** saved schedules, voice management, custom sounds
  and snooze. Shows also offer wake light and on-screen layouts.
- **[Bluetooth LE proxy and iBeacon](/features/bluetooth/):** relay BLE devices to Home Assistant and support room
  presence.

segments. Choose effects for listening, thinking, replies and calls; use room-reactive effects or
control the ring in automations.

[Buttons](/use/dot/#buttons) handle volume, mute, assistant activation, calls and alarm controls.

The Dot has the same [browser settings, alarms, calls and broadcasts](/use/web/) as screened

## Echo Show: native dashboards and Tessera

<div class="showcase flip">
<div>

The [native dashboard](/use/dashboards/) combines a clock and weather with tabs for lights,
switches, cameras, doorbells and your home alarm. Add photo wallpaper and choose the controls to
show.

[Rings](/use/dashboards/#rings) lays out your home as rings of floors, rooms, device types and
devices: tap to zoom in, and tap a device to control it.

[Tessera](/use/dashboards/#tessera) runs custom pages designed in Home Assistant, with climate,
energy, sensors, media and home-control tiles, drawn by Tessera's own screen software.

Pick [UI styles and themes](/use/styles/), [clock faces](/use/gallery/#clock-faces) and
[screen savers](/use/screen-saver/) with photos, clocks and audio-reactive visuals.

## Echo Show apps

<Image src={apps} alt="All Echo Show apps: Media, Alarms, Calendar, Intercom, Cameras, Doorbell, Photos, Home alarm, Lights, Switches, Mirror, Radar, Radio and Shopping list" />

[Apps](/features/apps/) include a calendar, shopping list, internet radio, animated weather radar,
lights and switches. Keep favourites in the dock. View Home Assistant cameras, open a photo
library, or use the built-in camera as a mirror.

The [Home alarm](/features/home-alarm/) app shows zones, a keypad and panel controls, with a choice
of designs and entry-delay alerts. [Doorbells](/features/doorbell/) have event history and
on-screen notifications. [Photos](/features/photos/) can come from Immich or Google Photos.

## Video calls, cameras and screen control

Shows add [video intercom](/features/intercom/) and [camera streaming](/features/camera/): RTSP,
ONVIF discovery and Home Assistant snapshots. Physical microphone mute also cuts the camera.

## Home Assistant integration and Fleet

ESPHome exposes settings, sensors, button events and playback. The optional
[EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/) adds device cards, a shared dashboard, Fleet comparison
and settings sync, wake-word and tone management, activity recordings, and voice actions for
calls, broadcasts and alarms. It supports [LANovo displays](https://www.lanovo.link) too.

## Interface gallery

The [interface gallery](/use/gallery/) groups dashboards, apps, media, photo settings, saved alarms,
home-alarm designs, calls, browser controls and Fleet. It includes clock faces, UI styles, themes,
message layouts, timer layouts and audio visuals, with names and links to their guides.

## Installation

You need Home Assistant, a supported Echo and USB access. Unlock the Echo first. Keep Fire OS
installed; EchoLocal uses its vendor hardware support.

1. [Identify the Echo and follow its unlock guide](/start/devices/).
2. [Prepare the device](/start/before/), then [install EchoLocal](/start/install/).
3. [Connect it to Home Assistant](/start/home-assistant/).

Updates and installation of older releases are available through Home Assistant and the
[control harness](/features/updates/), or from Settings on a Show.
